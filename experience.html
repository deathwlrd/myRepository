Job experiences

This powerplant was under a company called fluor. This job was very demanding as our shifts were 12 hours long with no days off. It helped me to stablish a strong work ethic. The installation of this panels was very intresting as I learned a lot about how solar energy works and how it is installed. This field was huge, and panels were not delicate at they were huge and any error could cause loss of this assets. My last construction job was located in New Orleans. We were hired to help with the construction of a multibillion dolalr powerplant that was going to use chemicals and process them to create eneryg. In this job site I developed a lot of skills as my craftmanship was changed multiple times. This was done by filling the pipes with air, nitrogen or water and pressurizing them to determine if any leaks were present. My last area was as a iron-worker, this are was pysically demanding as we had to set up structural steel and rebar for concrete. This area required a lot of precision as any error could cause misalignment of the structure. After this, I decided to change my career path and focus on computer science. In this job I am required to do multiple tasks such as helping professors with their work, organizing files, and any other task that is required.
